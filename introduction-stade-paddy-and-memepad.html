Introduction: Stade Paddy and Memepad

Published September 14, 2026

With version 0.2.6, we're introducing two new features: Paddy and Memepad. In short, they're simple soundpads. You can now share memes and audio clips from the Stade library directly in chats, groups, and Stadiums.

To use this feature after updating to the latest version, open any chat and tap the plus icon that replaced the old attachment paperclip (don't worry, media sharing is still there!). Pick either Memepad or Paddy, browse the library, and share away!

Note: Your Tor connection must be established to load the library. You can check the current status on the Transport Layers page.
